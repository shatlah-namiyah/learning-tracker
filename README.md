
# Learning Tracker

A simple, single-file learning tracker that runs in your browser. Organize what you're studying into projects, topics and subtopics, mark your progress, and keep track of how long things really take. No account, server or installation needed.

**Live version:** https://shatlah-namiyah.github.io/learning-tracker/

## Features

- **Learning projects:** a left-hand panel lists all your projects. A sample project is included to show how it works.
- **Topics and subtopics:** break a project into topics, and add subtopics under any topic. A topic with no subtopics works on its own.
- **Progress tracking:** click the circle next to an item to switch it between *not started*, *learning* and *done*.
- **Study time:** set an estimate for each item (15 minutes by default). Topics with subtopics automatically show the total time of their subtopics.
- **Stats:** a progress bar shows items done, percent complete, study time left and total study time.
- **Filters and "Up next":** filter by All, To do, Learning or Done, and see the next item to study.
- **Light and dark themes.**

## How to use

1. **Pick a project** from the "Learning projects" panel on the left.
2. **Track your progress** by clicking the circle beside an item. Click once for *learning*, twice for *done*, and a third time to reset it.
3. **Track real time:** change the minutes next to an item to how long it actually took you, so your totals reflect your true pace.
4. **Add a project:** click the **+** at the end of the project list. Enter a project title, add topics, and use **+ Subtopic** under any topic to add subtopics. Click **Save project** when done.
5. **Edit, reset or delete** a project with the buttons at the top of its page.
